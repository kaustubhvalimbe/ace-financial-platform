⛔ We Will NEVER…

  • Ask for your OTP, password, PIN or CVV — for any reason, ever.
  • Ask you to transfer money to a personal or private bank account. As an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor, your investments are processed only through official platforms (BSE StAR MF / NSE NMF II) and go directly to the mutual fund — never to us personally.
  • Promise guaranteed, fixed or assured returns. Mutual fund returns are market-linked and never guaranteed.
  • Pressure you to act urgently, invest "before a deadline," or join a "special profit group."
  • Contact you from a phone number or email other than the official ones listed above.

If any message asks for the above — it is a fraud attempt, even if it uses our name, logo or ARN. Please ignore it and verify with us directly.

⚠️ Common Fraud Red Flags

  • A WhatsApp or Telegram account using our name/photo but a different number.
  • "Investment groups" or "stock tips" channels claiming to be run by us, promising quick profits.
  • Emails from look-alike domains (e.g. a misspelled version of acefinservices.com).
  • Requests to click a link and "log in" or "verify" by entering bank/card details.
  • Any request for payment to a UPI ID, wallet, or personal account.
  • Anyone guaranteeing returns or "doubling" your money.

🛡️ If You Receive a Suspicious Message

  • Do not reply, click any link, share any OTP/password, or make any payment.
