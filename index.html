CORPORATE TREASURY
Make Your Idle Surplus Work
Most companies keep working-capital surplus in a current account — where it earns nothing at all. Liquid and short-duration debt funds offer a way to put that money to work without giving up access to it.

The cost of an idle current account

A current account pays no interest — that is how they are designed. For a business holding surplus between receivables and payables, that money simply sits still while inflation erodes it. Liquid funds were built precisely for this gap: a place to park short-term surplus that stays accessible, typically within one working day, while still earning a return.

The question is never “fund or current account” for money you need tomorrow morning. It is: how much of your balance genuinely needs to sit idle, and how much could be working? That is the conversation we have with every corporate client.

💧

Liquid Funds

For surplus you may need within days or weeks. Invest in very short-maturity instruments, with redemption proceeds typically credited the next working day. Several schemes offer instant redemption within prescribed limits.

⏳

Ultra-Short & Low Duration

For surplus with a three to twelve month horizon — advance tax provisioning, planned capex, bonus provisions. Slightly longer maturity profiles in exchange for potentially better yield than overnight parking.

🔓

Liquidity Without Lock-In

Unlike a fixed deposit, there is no tenure to commit to and no premature-withdrawal penalty. Redeem part of your holding and leave the rest invested — useful when working-capital needs are uneven.

🧾

Tax Timing Advantage

Fixed deposit interest is taxed as it accrues, year after year, and attracts TDS. Gains on mutual fund units are taxed only when you actually redeem, and resident investors face no TDS on redemption — so tax follows the cash, not the calendar.

📊

Laddering Your Surplus

We help map your cash-flow cycle and split surplus across horizons — overnight, weekly, quarterly — so each rupee sits in the right place for how long you actually need it.

🤝

Employee Benefits

Beyond treasury, we arrange group health and life cover for your team, and help employees make sense of their own investments through workplace sessions.

A note on tax, stated plainly: indexation benefit on debt mutual funds was withdrawn for units acquired on or after 1 April 2023. Gains on such units are taxed at the applicable rate regardless of how long they are held. We mention this because you may still see indexation quoted elsewhere — it no longer applies. The advantages above are about liquidity, yield on idle balances, and when tax falls due, not about avoiding tax. Please confirm treatment for your company with your Chartered Accountant.
