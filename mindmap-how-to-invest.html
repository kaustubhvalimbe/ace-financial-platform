10
Processing & NAV allotment
For offline, the cheque is sent to the respective AMC and typically processed within about two days. Once the payment is cleared, NAV is allotted per the applicable cut-off rules, and your Folio is generated.
Subject to AMFI / AMC cut-off timing rules
11
Access your account & first statement
Once the Folio is generated (usually within a day or two), you receive your login credentials. Log in to the client portal to view your holdings and download your first Account Statement.
Your portfolio, always accessible
