Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about comparing mutual funds
How do I compare two mutual funds before investing?
Use the Fund Compare tool above to place up to 4 mutual funds side-by-side on returns (6M, 1Y, 3Y, 5Y, Since Inception), NAV, expense ratio, risk level, and category rank — making it easy to see which fund actually fits your goals rather than relying on a single number.
What should I check when comparing mutual funds — returns or expense ratio?
Both matter, but for the same time period. A fund with slightly lower returns but a much lower expense ratio can outperform over the long term once costs compound. This tool shows both side-by-side so you can weigh them together rather than looking at returns alone.
Is a lower expense ratio always better?
Not automatically — expense ratio matters most when comparing funds with similar returns and risk within the same category. A fund with a higher expense ratio can still be the better choice if its risk-adjusted performance justifies the cost. Compare funds within the same category for a fair read.
