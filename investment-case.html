How STP Cycles work:
Each cycle parks a lumpsum in a Liquid Fund, then systematically transfers to one or more Equity Funds. Each equity transfer is an independent lumpsum entry that grows for its own period from its own transfer date. Multiple cycles allow you to model different AMC strategies in a single plan.
